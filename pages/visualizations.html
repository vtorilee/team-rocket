What are Pokemon Made of?

The Breakdown of Pokemon Types

Every Pokémon has a type — and sometimes even two! From pure Fire or Water types to unique dual-type combinations like Grass/Poison or Steel/Fairy, typing plays a huge role in how Pokémon behave, battle, and are categorized.

This sunburst visualization explores the breakdown of Pokémon types, starting with primary (first) types at the core and expanding outward to show how those types combine with secondary types. It reveals both the most common type pairings and the rarer combinations that make certain Pokémon stand out.

Data from: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/guavocado/pokemon-stats-1025-pokemons?resource=download

Expanding the Pokedex!

The Growth of the Number of Pokemon Over the 9 Generations

Every generation brings a new wave of Pokémon into the world! From the original 151 in Gen 1 to the most recent additions in Gen 9, each region introduces its own unique mix of new species for trainers to discover and catch.

This visualization shows the total number of Pokémon introduced in each generation, highlighting how the franchise has grown and evolved over time with both large expansions and smaller, more focused additions.

These values are based on official Pokémon generation data across all nine regions.
