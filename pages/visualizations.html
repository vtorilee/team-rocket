Total Pokémon by Generation

Every generation brings a new wave of Pokémon into the world! From the original 151 in Gen 1 to the most recent additions in Gen 9, each region introduces its own unique mix of new species for trainers to discover and catch. This visualization shows the total number of Pokémon introduced in each generation, highlighting how the franchise has grown and evolved over time with both large expansions and smaller, more focused additions. These values are based on official Pokémon generation data across all nine regions.

Starter Pokémon Evolution

Every journey starts somewhere! For many trainers, the first Pokemon on their journey is chosen from one of 3 starter Pokemon. Varying on the region, trainers can choose from a fire, water or grass starter to accompany them along their journey! This visualizations reveals the popularity of the starters overall from the 9 generations.
